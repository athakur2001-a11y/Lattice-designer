How these numbers are made

      
Each cell type is a periodic network of round struts. Porosity, surface area, largest pore and throat were computed numerically for an infinite lattice (about a million sample points per type, so about ±0.3 % porosity). Specimen values multiply the per-cell values by the envelope volume and ignore edge effects such as strut stubs on the outside. Struts are modelled as round and joined at the nodes; NX tubes have flat ends, so NX's Measure Body can differ by about 1 %. The NX script and file-size estimates come from your BCC and diamond runs (1,000 BCC cells ≈ 2 min and 41 MB). Cells you add are calculated in your browser the same way with fewer sample points: porosity to about ±0.3 %, largest pore and throat to about ±1 % of the cell size. STL files are measured as they are; their strut diameter is estimated as 4 × volume ÷ surface, which assumes round struts.
